# Clinical and Localization Review Items

This document tracks clinical signs, terminology, and colloquial translations that require review and formal sign-off by a qualified Medical Officer (PHC/CHC) and native Marathi/Hindi speakers prior to full state-wide field rollout.

## 1. Clinical Signs for Verification Sign-Off

All core signs in ASHA Sahayak derive from official NHM Modules 6 & 7, HBNC Guidelines, and IMNCI. The following specific items should be validated in local clinical context:

| Module | Sign ID | Clinical Question | Recommended Reviewer | Current Verification Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newborn | `nb_umbilicus_red_pus` | Umbilicus red with redness spreading to skin or pus | Pediatrician / Neonatologist | Verified against HBNC Section 3 |
| Newborn | `nb_temp_abnormal` | Fever (>= 37.5°C) or Hypothermia (< 35.5°C) | Pediatrician | Verified against HBNC Section 3 |
| Newborn | `nb_jaundice_early_soles` | Jaundice in first 24h or yellow palms/soles | Neonatologist | Verified against IMNCI Young Infant chart |
| Pregnancy | `preg_convulsions_fits` | Fits or loss of consciousness during pregnancy | Gynecologist / Obstetrician | Verified against NHM Book 6 Eclampsia protocol |
| Pregnancy | `preg_reduced_movements` | Fetal movements reduced or absent (> 28 weeks) | Obstetrician | Verified against PMSMA high-risk protocol |
| Pregnancy | `preg_labour_over_12h` | Labor pains lasting > 12 hours | Medical Officer | Verified against NHM Prolonged Labor standard |
| Child | `ch_chest_indrawing_stridor` | Chest indrawing or stridor when calm | Pediatrician | Verified against IMNCI Severe Pneumonia |
| Child | `ch_severe_dehydration` | Sunken eyes + skin pinch > 2 seconds | Pediatrician | Verified against IMNCI Diarrhea chart |
| Child | `ch_fast_breathing` | 2-11m >= 50 bpm; 12-59m >= 40 bpm | Pediatrician | Verified against IMNCI Fast Breathing chart |
| Adult | `ad_cough_2w` | Cough lasting 2 weeks or more | Chest Physician / NTEP Officer | Verified against NTEP Presumptive TB guidelines |
| Adult | `ad_fever_chills_vector` | High fever with chills, body ache in vector season | Epidemiologist / NVBDCP | Verified against NVBDCP Malaria/Dengue triage |

## 2. Localization and Colloquial Phrasing for Native-Speaker Review

ASHAs frequently use colloquial rural idioms rather than formal Sanskritized vocabulary. The following phrases should be reviewed with active ASHA facilitators in Pune, Gadchiroli, and Nashik:

### Marathi (मराठी) Terms
- **"झटके / आकडी"**: Used for convulsions/fits in infants and pregnant women. Review if "फेफरे" or "आचके" is preferred locally.
- **"छाती आत खेचली जात आहे"**: Used for subcostal chest indrawing. Ensure rural mothers and ASHAs distinguish this clearly from rapid stomach breathing.
- **"कळा सुरू होण्यापूर्वीच पाणी सुटणे"**: Premature rupture of membranes (PROM). Review if "अंगावरून पाणी जाणे" is clearer.
- **"सुस्त किंवा बेशुद्ध"**: Lethargic or unresponsive. Review if "बाळ डोळे उघडत नाही / निपचित पडले आहे" communicates urgency better.
- **"अंगावर पुळ्या किंवा मोठे फोड"**: Skin pustules/blisters. Confirm alignment with local terms like "फोड" or "पुळी".

### Hindi (हिंदी) Terms
- **"दौरे / झटके पड़ना"**: Convulsions / fits.
- **"छाती का अंदर धंसना"**: Severe chest indrawing.
- **"प्रसव पीड़ा 12 घंटे से अधिक चलना"**: Prolonged labor > 12 hours.
- **"त्वचा की चुटकी बहुत धीरे वापस जाना"**: Skin pinch test for severe dehydration.
- **"खांसी के साथ खून आना"**: Hemoptysis / blood in sputum.

## 3. Disclaimers and Protocols
- Triage Disclaimer in Marathi: *"हे ॲप केवळ निर्णय साहाय्यासाठी आहे, हा डॉक्टरांचा अंतिम निदान नाही. शंका असल्यास त्वरित आपल्या एएनएम (ANM) किंवा प्राथमिक आरोग्य केंद्राशी (PHC) संपर्क साधा."*
- Triage Disclaimer in Hindi: *"यह ऐप केवल निर्णय सहायता के लिए है, यह डॉक्टर की अंतिम जांच नहीं है। संदेह होने पर तुरंत अपनी एएनएम (ANM) या प्राथमिक स्वास्थ्य केंद्र (PHC) से संपर्क करें।"*
- Triage Disclaimer in English: *"This tool provides clinical decision support only, not a medical diagnosis. When in doubt, contact your ANM or Primary Health Centre immediately."*
